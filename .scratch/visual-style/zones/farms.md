# Farms

Part of the [visual-style spec](../spec.md), which keeps this Zone's one-line description (Block fill) and everything the Zones share.

## What it builds

### Stages 1 to 3

- Farms: a cottage and a barn with one bale and no silo, low shoots in most fields and about a third left fallow; then a farmhouse with a veranda, a silo and every field grown; then a two-storey farmhouse, a second, shorter silo and a lean-to, fuller rows 45% wider, and a few pines behind the house where there is room.
  Their details are below.

### Built to the Finish

- **Farms built to the Finish** ([#184](https://github.com/artsaraiva/dioramania/issues/184), `src/render/zones/farms.ts`, `src/render/movers.ts`), the whole of its menu.
  The kit's draws are as on `main` (the farmstead's chance, each field's crop, fallow and stream), and each field is still what #149 drew from where it lies; what #184 adds is drawn after those, or from a stream of the Block's own.
  - Ground: the Block is trodden earth, and each field lies on soil of its own in furrows 0.4 m apart that run with its rows: pale under crops and greenhouses, dark where it is freshly tilled or lies fallow at Stage 1, straw-pale stubble where it is cut, mown grass under an orchard. Surface pattern on the shared material; the soil pattern takes a quarter turn where the rows run north–south.
  - Headlands: rows stop 4.5 m short of the Block's ends, 3 m short of a farmyard or a scene and 2 m beside one, which leaves a lane 1.5 m beyond their ends on the earth, where a tractor turns clear of the pavement.
  - An irrigation channel in the place of the first row, where that row is 10 m or longer: a concrete trough 1 m wide of the paddies' pale water along one edge of the Block, with a sluice across its middle, two posts, a gate and its wheel (Block zoom).
  - Kinds of field, each drawn from where the field lies, as shares of the fields that are neither paddies nor under greenhouses: an orchard (13%), fruit trees every 4.5 m along every other row, saplings at Stage 1, grown at Stage 2 and in fruit at Stage 3; a cut field of round bales (11%), two, four, then six of them, two of the six wrapped in white film; a cut field of rice drying on racks (9%), one, two, then three, each a pole on crossed legs hung with sheaves; a tilled field (20%), dark soil bare at Stage 1 and then under low shoots; the rest crops as before. An orchard, bales or racks need three rows 10 m long.
  - The farmstead, on the Blocks that had one, in a yard 22 m along the Block by 16 m across, its barn's door toward the fields. On the common 34 m Block it stands in the middle with rows only beside it.
    A farmhouse of its own (it was low-density's house): plaster walls with a framed window in every bay, lit at night, under a hipped roof in tile rows, a lantern by its door; a cottage at Stage 1, from Stage 2 the house with a veranda along its front (a timber deck under a lean-to roof on posts), at Stage 3 a second storey over a ledge.
    A barn 7 m by 9 m: corrugated walls with a high strip of windows, lit at night, under a ribbed gable roof, its door in the gable end 3.6 m wide, a hay loft across its back, a lamp over the door, and round bales behind it under a lean-to at Stage 3. Its silos are welded plates with a ladder up each. Where a tractor parks in it, its door's leaves are folded back against its front; where none does, they are shut.
    A well, a ring of stone over water under a little roof on two posts; a chicken run, wire mesh on posts round trodden straw, with a coop on legs; the kei truck and its crates as #149 had them.
  - The scenes: the farmstead is its Block's scene, from Stage 1. A Block without one has an orchard or a roadside produce stand from Stage 2, picked from where the Block lies, and a Block of 2,500 m² or more the other, or one of the two beside its farmstead, at Stage 3. Each takes its ground before the fields are laid, at an end of the Block or in a corner of it, so that the rows behind it only start later.
    An orchard, 12 m by 9 m: mown grass under two rows of four fruit trees, saplings until its Stage, then in fruit with a picker by a ladder against one, a stack of crates, a wheelbarrow and a lantern on a post; at Stage 3 taller trees, a second ladder and more crates.
    A produce stand, 7 m by 4.2 m, facing a Road where the Block has one: a timber counter on setts, bare until its Stage, then under a tiled roof on posts with crates of produce, a lantern, a nobori, a price board and an honesty box on a post (Block zoom); at Stage 3 a second nobori, more crates, pumpkins and a bench.
    On the 26-Tile City: 26 farmsteads, 33 orchards and 38 stands on 71 Blocks.
  - What moves, from Stage 2: tractors, egrets and chickens.
    A tractor works two rows side by side of a tilled field, with nothing beside them or beyond their ends but that field's low shoots, which it straddles, in whole laps so that it ends each day where it set out; a field of crops is tilled for it where no tilled one will do. A second works another tilled field at Stage 3. 45 tractors at Stage 2 and 48 at Stage 3.

The field it reaches from the barn is tilled whatever it drew. 17 of the 26 barns have theirs; the other nine have no two rows a tractor can reach, and stand shut.
    Egrets (a new mover): one in three paddies in four from Stage 2 and a second in two in five at Stage 3. Each stands, takes a step or two, and flies to another spot of its paddy, wings beating. 56 and 89.

    A stand's nobori flutter, as shops' do.
  - Night: the greenhouses' film is lit from inside and leaves its light round each house, farms' night signature; the farmhouse's windows and its lantern, the barn's strip of windows and the lamp over its door, the tractor in the barn, the stand's lantern under its roof and the orchard's on its post, each with its pool.

### Departures, gaps and what is beyond the menu

For the user to accept or drop with the images:

- Departures and gaps: a tractor works two rows of one tilled field rather than touring every field of crops, since its path must keep clear of what stands (the check's paths rule), and the shoots it straddles are low; nine barns of 26 have no tractor; on a farmstead's Block the field by the barn is tilled even where #149 drew a paddy or greenhouses; the farmhouse is farms' own, no longer low-density's house; the barn's door is in its gable end, and its walls are sheet where they were flat colour; the first row of most Blocks gives way to the channel; rows are 1.5 m shorter at each end for the headland.
- Required by the rules rather than the menu: the headland lane and the rows' room round a yard or a scene (paths and keeps-clear); the lantern by the farmhouse's door, the barn's lamp, the orchard's lantern and the stand's, with their pools (rule 7); the barn's floor and the run's straw, which cover their contact shadows.
- Beyond the menu: the silos' ladders and plates; the hay loft and the lean-to's bales; wrapped bales; the stand's nobori, price board, pumpkins and bench; the orchard's picker, wheelbarrow and lantern; the chicken coop.

### Details

- **Farm details** ([#149](https://github.com/artsaraiva/dioramania/issues/149), `src/render/zones/farms.ts`), each field's drawn from where it lies, so no other field and nothing else on its Block changes:
  - About one field in five is a rice paddy at every Stage: pale aqua water inside a low bank, under two rows of shoots to each row of crop, 0.16, 0.34 and 0.46 m tall at Stages 1 to 3.
    A paddy left fallow at Stage 1 is flooded and bare.
  - About one field in eight goes under vinyl greenhouses at Stage 2, and one in five at Stage 3: long pale arched tunnels, 3.5 m across and 2.3 m high, two side by side over a field and at most 26 m long, with a hoop every 2.4 m and a door at each end.
    They stop a metre short of the rows' ends.
  - A scarecrow stands in a paddy, three in ten from Stage 1, more than half from Stage 2 and three in four at Stage 3, and in a few fields of crops from Stage 2 (one in twenty) and Stage 3 (one in seven): a post with outstretched arms in a bright shirt, a cloth head and a wide straw hat, about 3 m tall.
  - A kei truck stands in a farmstead's yard from Stage 2, on the side the default camera sees, with two crates on its flat bed; at Stage 3 the bed is full, and a stack of four crates stands beside it.
  - Tractors work a tilled field (since #184, above; #149's toured every field of crops): one a Block from Stage 2, and a second at Stage 3 where a second tilled field has room.
    A tractor drives up one row and back down the next, straddling each, and turns on the headland beyond their ends.
    It drives at 2.8 m/s, oversized as the cars are, and bobs a little as it goes.

### Farm details ([#149](https://github.com/artsaraiva/dioramania/issues/149))

Farm details ([#149](https://github.com/artsaraiva/dioramania/issues/149)), every Tile of the 26-Tile City a farm at Stage 3, cost 0.46 ms (6.72 against `main`'s 6.26), for six more draw calls and 0.34 M more triangles.
Their 106 tractors take 0.04 ms a frame to place and about 0.1 ms to draw: the rest is the greenhouses and the paddies' shoots.

## Amended by [#149](https://github.com/artsaraiva/dioramania/issues/149) (2026-11-17)

Farms carry details of their own, picked by the user from a sheet of Stages 1 to 3: rice paddies, vinyl greenhouses, scarecrows, a kei truck in the farmyard, and tractors that work the fields.
The tractors are the first moving details, and build the machinery every Zone's will use: drawn instanced outside the merged scenery, arriving with a Rebuild's bricks and leaving with their Tile, at work through the day and at rest by night (Block fill, below).
